introduction to web-designing and development

Web design is the process of creating the visual and functional elements of a website, focusing on both its appearance and user experience.

It involves planning the layout, colors, typography, and imagery, and also includes user interface (UI) and user experience (UX) design to ensure a website is intuitive and easy to navigate across all devices.

Key aspects include aesthetics, usability, accessibility, and search engine optimization (SEO).
